Aussie Hoops

Ages 5 to 10

Where every basketball journey starts. Fun, safe and all about falling in love with the game.

Ford Aussie Hoops is Basketball Australia's official junior program, run locally by the Launceston Basketball Association. It gives kids a fun, safe first taste of basketball while building skills, confidence and friendships that last well beyond the court.

  • For 5 to 10 year olds, no experience needed
  • 8 week program every school term
  • Led by nationally accredited coaches

What they'll learn

More than just basketball

Each session mixes skills, games and teamwork so every child leaves with a smile and something new.

Core skills

Running, bouncing, dribbling, passing and shooting, taught through games kids love.

Teamwork

Weekly team-building and social activities that help kids make friends and work together.

Confidence

A positive, encouraging space where every child gets touches on the ball and feels part of the team.

Healthy habits

An active, inclusive start that sets kids up for a lifetime of sport and community.

Got questions?

FAQs

Everything parents ask before their child's first session.

Does my child need any experience?

Not at all. Aussie Hoops is designed for kids who are brand new to basketball, as well as those who have had a bounce in the backyard.

What age is it for?

Aussie Hoops is for children aged 5 to 10 years old.

How long does the program run?

Each program runs for 8 weeks, starting in the first week of the school term. You can join any term.

Aussie Hoops

Aussie Hoops provides children with a fun and safe Basketball experience that serves as an introduction to a lifetime of involvement in the game. In addition to promoting healthy and active lifestyles, the program serves as a platform for social development and inclusion – fostering stronger and more vibrant communities and individuals. Suited for 5-10 year olds starting their basketball journey.

Runs every school term, commencing in the first week of school for 8 weeks.

For more information and sessions, please see www.aussiehoops.basketball
