FAQs

Everything you need to know before you hit the court. Can't find your answer? Send us an enquiry.

Do I need to join a club?

For most competitions, yes. You play for one of our seven member clubs. Junior Social and Senior Social are the exceptions, where anyone can enter a team.

What does it cost?

Every player needs a Basketball Tasmania registration, valid for 12 months, plus a team fee for each game. Social competitions start from $49 per match. Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet.

How do I register?

Registrations are done online through PlayHQ. Clubs register their teams, and social teams can register directly using the links on each competition.

I have never played before. Where do I start?

Aussie Hoops is perfect for 5 to 10 year olds, and Junior Social is a relaxed first step for older kids. For adults, Senior Social is a great way in.
